## Applications
- **Sky Lift:** Hydraulic access platform with 11 m and 15 m max working height options and GEM portal availability.
- **Road Sweeper:** 3 CUM hopper, 600 L water tank, 1 main brush and 2 side brushes with hydraulic hopper unloading.
- **Closed Garbage Tipper:** 4–8 CUM capacity with 70:30 or 60:40 wet/dry compartmentalization and single or twin tipping.
- **Open Garbage Tipper:** Customizable 4–6 CUM capacity with best-in-class turning circle radius.
- **Garbage Compactor:** 4 CUM customizable capacity with automatic bin lifter for municipal waste collection.
- **Water Tanker:** 3000 litre tank capacity for municipal water supply and civic utility use.
- **Suction & Jetting Machine:** 3 KL tank with 1.5 KL fresh water and 1.5 KL sludge, jetting pump 137 lpm at 140 bar.
- **Fire Fighter:** 3 KL tank capacity fire fighter body for municipal and disaster response fleets.
- **Hook Loader:** 4 CUM garbage collection with 3500 mm container length and max lifting capacity of 2 tonnes.
- **Other Applications:** Fire fighting truck with 3 KL tanker, mortuary van, cattle catcher and mobile food truck configurations.
