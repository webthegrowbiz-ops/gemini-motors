Common questions

Which LCV should I choose?

It depends on your load, route and deck preference. Gemini Motors can help you compare the DOST range, SAATHI and other approved LCV options for Goa delivery work.

What's the on-road price in Goa?

On-road price depends on the selected LCV model, body/load configuration, and current RTO and insurance charges. Share your requirement above and we'll send an exact, itemised quote - usually within one business day.

Do you offer EMI or financing?

Yes — flexible EMI options through partner banks and NBFCs. The calculator above gives a rough estimate; your exact EMI depends on tenure, down payment and your eligibility.

Is service available across Goa?

Yes, Gemini Motors supports North Goa and South Goa customers through one confirmed central contact number: +91 94223 93288.

Are you an authorised Ashok Leyland dealer?

Yes, Gemini Motors is an authorised Ashok Leyland dealership. Quotes reflect current ex-showroom pricing plus applicable RTO and insurance charges at the time of booking.
